Melbourne, 2004. The underworld war between the Lygon Street Crew and the Broadmeadows mob is in its sixth year, Taskforce Kestrel is closing in, and you just got off the plane at Tullamarine.

On foot

WASD move, Shift run, mouse aims, Click or Space shoots, Q or 1–4 swaps weapon.

Driving

W/S gas and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, E get in or out, H horn. Keep left, this is Melbourne.

Touch

Drag the left side to move or steer. FIRE, CAR, BRAKE and GUN sit on the right.

The city

Pink M markers start jobs. M opens the map. Ammu-Nation sells guns, the Mod Shop clears your stars, safe houses save.
