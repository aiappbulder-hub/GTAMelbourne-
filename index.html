Same city. Different rules.

Melbourne, 2004. The underworld war between the Lygon Street Crew and the Broadmeadows mob is in its sixth year, Taskforce Kestrel is closing in, and you just got off the plane at Tullamarine.

On foot

WASD move (in the chase camera A/D turn you), Shift run, mouse aims, Click or Space shoots, Q or 1–4 swaps weapon.

Driving

W gas, S brake and reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake (HB on touch), E get in or out, H horn. Keep left, this is Melbourne.

Touch

Drag the left side to walk and the right side to look around. In a car, use the ◀ ▶ arrows to steer, GAS to go, BRAKE to stop and REV to reverse. FIRE, CAR, BRAKE and GUN sit on the right.

The city

Turn the camera with Z/C or by right-dragging. Pink M markers start jobs. M opens the map, V switches between the 3D, chase and 2D cameras. Ammu-Nation sells guns, the Mod Shop clears your stars, safe houses save.

Paused

GTA driving: W or stick up for gas, S or stick down to brake and reverse, A/D to steer, Space for the handbrake. Point driving steers toward the direction you hold, in the overhead and 2D views.
